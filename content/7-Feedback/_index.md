### 1. Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The FCAJ office (26th floor, Bitexco) is very open and comfortable. Flexible working hours help me manage my work schedule efficiently.

**2. Support from Mentor and Team Admin**  
Mentors are very attentive; they frequently check in on our project progress and provide enthusiastic guidance.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
Very relevant. As a Computer Science student, the tasks align with core fundamentals and allow me to practice on real-world cloud technologies like AWS, Terraform, Docker, and CI/CD.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
The internship helps me develop both technical skills (AWS deployment, GitHub Actions, Terraform, etc.) and soft skills (communication, teamwork, progress reporting).

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
The company culture is respectful, friendly, and emphasizes strong team spirit. Everyone is always ready to share and help each other hit deadlines regardless of hierarchy.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
Support for flexible working hours matching university schedules and opportunities to participate in weekend sharing/training sessions.

### 2. Detailed Q&A Response

* **What did you find most satisfying during your internship?**  
  > What satisfied me most was interacting and learning alongside a dynamic student community, while receiving close guidance from mentors, which made me much more confident and proactive.

* **What do you think the company should improve for future interns?**  
  > The company has provided great support. If possible, increasing the number of on-site working sessions per week would give interns more opportunities to interact and communicate directly with the team.

* **If recommending to a friend, would you suggest they intern here? Why?**  
  > **Definitely yes.** FCAJ is a fantastic internship environment for IT/CS students who want hands-on experience with Cloud Computing and the AWS ecosystem.
